This week on Vintage Politics, I’m joined by comedian, satirist and social media sensation Rosie Holt, best known for her brilliantly convincing political parody videos.

We talk about how she became an unlikely political commentator, the politicians who inspire her characters, and why political satire can sometimes feel almost impossible when real life is quite so ridiculous.

Over three very different wines, we get to know the woman behind the comedy, talk about her career, the power of satire and the challenges of making people laugh in today’s political climate.

And, of course, we finish with a wine from Rosie’s birth year. Can she guess her vintage?
