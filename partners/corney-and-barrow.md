Founded in 1780, Corney & Barrow is one of the UK's oldest independent wine merchants. 75% of its portfolio comprises exclusive wines, reflecting the strength of its long-standing producer relationships. The Corney & Barrow range encompasses wines of the highest quality, from en primeur offerings and everyday drinking wines to some of the world's most sought-after fine wines.

To celebrate our partnership with Corney & Barrow, you’ll soon be able to order a limited-edition case featuring wines from the show. Look out for further details in the run-up to Christmas.
